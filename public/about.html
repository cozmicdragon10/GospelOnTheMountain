About

Our Mission

Worship On The Mountain exists to bring people together, regardless of denomination, for a day of worship, music and thanksgiving to our Lord in Heaven, and to bring hope to everyone who struggles with their mental health.

We are gathering worship groups, choirs, bands and volunteers to put on a festival and revival where every church and every person is welcome. He said He can and will move mountains for each one of us. Together, we want to shake one for Him.

Mental health is close to our hearts. Our organizer, Samuel Wright Jr., has walked this road himself, from foster homes to inpatient facilities, and no one should be afraid to ask for help. The proceeds from this event will go toward mental health care, to a place where they can truly change the situation for people in need, such as patients at Peninsula Health in Knoxville. We are still prayerfully choosing exactly where.

Event Organizer

Hey, my name is Samuel Kenneth Wright Jr. I'm a 38-year-old gospel country music artist from Lewisburg, Tennessee. I grew up tobacco farming, then worked in welding and fabrication.

I've been called by the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ to lead men, and I don't understand why He's chosen me, but here I am, figuring out each and every step of the way exactly why He chose me. I hope you follow along to find out.

I'm a father, a brother, an uncle, a cousin, a friend, a husband, and most of all, a man of God.

Mental health is something that I struggle with myself. Some of it is genetics, and some of it is trauma. My journey isn't over. I'm terrified that I might have to go back to a place like that just to get help, and I want to take that terrified feeling away from everybody. I've seen things firsthand, from foster homes to inpatient facilities. I have a lot of insight, and I look forward to sharing it with the world.
