Billy Graham Prayer Line: Call 855-255-PRAY (7729) to reach someone who will pray with you, 24/7.

Mental health crisis? Text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line (US), or call 911 if someone is in immediate danger.

Veterans in crisis? Call 988 and press 1, or text 838255 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line (US), free and available 24/7.

Thinking about suicide, or worried about someone? You are not alone. Call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US), free and available 24/7.
